Nightlife FAQ

Before you send the request.

Can you work with very dark venues?

Yes. Nightlife and low-light spaces are a core part of the Hynoe Flicks style. Venue conditions still matter, so include the venue when you book.

Do you only take posed photos?

No. Posed frames can be part of the set, but the room, people and movement are part of the coverage too.

How is nightlife pricing handled?

It is quoted around the date, venue, amount of coverage and intended use instead of using a made-up one-size package.
