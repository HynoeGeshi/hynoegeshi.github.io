FAQ

Know before you book.

The things people usually need answered before sending a request.

Do you help with posing and direction?

Yes. Direction is part of the process, especially for portraits. The goal is to give enough guidance to look intentional without making the session feel stiff.

Can you shoot in clubs, venues and other low-light spaces?

Yes. Nightlife and low-light environments are a core part of the Hynoe Flicks look.

Can I hire you only for editing?

Yes. Use the Editing Only option in the booking flow and include how many photos you have plus a reference for the finish you want.

Why is pricing listed as a custom quote?

Different locations, coverage needs and deliverables change the scope. The booking form gathers those details first so the quote can match the actual job instead of a vague package.

How do I send inspiration?

The booking flow has a space for reference links and notes. Instagram, Pinterest or a moodboard link works well.

Ready when you are

Show me the vision. We’ll build the frame around it.

Send the date, location and what you want the photos to feel like. The request takes a couple of short steps and keeps your answers if submission ever fails.
