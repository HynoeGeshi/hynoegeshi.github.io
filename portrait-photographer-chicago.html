Portrait FAQ

You do not need to know how to pose.

No. Direction is part of the session, and the goal is to give you something useful to do instead of telling you to “act natural.”

Yes. The booking form has a reference-link field so you can send the mood, light, outfit or framing you are aiming for.

Are edits included?

The final scope is confirmed with your quote. Hynoe Flicks portrait work is presented with an edited finish; exact quantities are not invented before the session is defined.
